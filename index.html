I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Institute of Statistics and Big Data, Renmin University of China in 2026, supervised by Prof. Wei Ma. From January to May 2026, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the MRC Biostatistics Unit, University of Cambridge, hosted by Dr. David Robertson. I am collaborating with Dr. Stef Baas, Dr. David Robertson, and Prof. Sofia Villar on exact tests for covariate-adjusted response-adaptive designs. I am also fortunate to collaborate with Prof. Hanzhong Liu (Tsinghua University) and Prof. Feifang Hu (The George Washington University).

Prior to this, I received my Bachelor's degree in Statistics from the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China in 2021.

Research Interests

My research interests lie in biostatistics, precision and personalized medicine, and the design and analysis of clinical trials. Specifically, I am interested in:

  • Biostatistics

  • Adaptive Clinical Trial Design

  • Precision and Personalized Medicine

  • Causal Inference

  • Robust Inference
